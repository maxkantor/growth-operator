# YouTube Safe Engagement

Build an actionable plan that creates genuine viewer conversations and improves discovery without manufacturing metrics.

## Establish context

1. Inspect any attached YouTube Studio screenshots or analytics.
2. Extract the video topic, language, publish date, views, comments, and any visible performance signals.
3. Use known channel context when available. For Max Kantor Cooking, default to bilingual English/Russian copy unless the user requests one language.
4. Ask only for information that materially changes the plan. Proceed with sensible assumptions when the title or screenshot provides enough context.
5. Browse current official YouTube Help documentation before making claims about engagement, spam, monetization, or platform rules. Prefer primary YouTube/Google sources and cite them in the answer.

## Apply the authenticity test

Treat an action as legitimate only when a human user's primary intent is authentic interaction with the content.

Allow occasional use of a second account only as a genuine viewer:

- Watch normally once when interested or reviewing the upload.
- Like once if the viewer genuinely likes the video.
- Leave one specific, topic-relevant comment.
- Continue the conversation only when a reply adds substance.

Do not recommend or perform:

- Repeated playback to increase views or watch time.
- A routine of liking and commenting on every upload from the same alternate account.
- Multiple alternate accounts, coordinated engagement groups, sub-for-sub, or rewards for engagement.
- Generic, repetitive, copied, deceptive, automated, or purchased comments, likes, subscribers, views, or watch time.
- Attempts to disguise ownership, evade detection, simulate independent customers, or guarantee algorithmic results.

Explain the boundary plainly without accusing or lecturing the user. State that one extra interaction has negligible distribution impact and redirect effort toward real audience acquisition.

## Create the engagement package

When asked for an approach to a specific upload, provide:

1. **Viewer comment:** Write one natural question or observation tied to a specific moment, ingredient, technique, or result in the video. Avoid generic praise.
2. **Creator reply:** Answer helpfully and end with an easy follow-up question that invites real viewers to join.
3. **Pinned creator comment:** Ask one low-friction, topic-specific question. Do not falsely present the creator as an independent viewer.
4. **Community post:** Draft a short teaser with a genuine question and video link placeholder.
5. **External share copy:** Draft one non-spammy message appropriate for a relevant cooking, family, or social audience. Recommend sharing only where allowed and genuinely relevant.
6. **Short-form hook:** Suggest one Short or clip that can send interested viewers to the full video.

Generate English and Russian versions when the audience or video is bilingual. Keep translations natural rather than literal.

## Build the rollout

Use this default sequence unless analytics justify a change:

- **At publication:** Add the pinned comment and verify title, thumbnail, description, end screen, cards, captions, and playlist placement.
- **First 24 hours:** Publish one Community post, reply promptly to every genuine comment, and share selectively to relevant audiences.
- **Days 2–3:** Publish one strong Short using the best visual payoff or technique and point interested viewers to the full recipe.
- **After sufficient data:** Compare impressions, click-through rate, first-30-second retention, average view duration, traffic sources, and returning viewers. Recommend changing the thumbnail/title only when the evidence supports it.

Do not claim that a fixed delay before liking or commenting tricks the algorithm. Do not promise daily customers, views, ranking, or monetization.

## Prioritize by impact

Rank recommendations in this order:

1. Thumbnail and title click appeal.
2. Opening retention and pacing.
3. Relevant distribution through Shorts, Community, search, playlists, and external audiences.
4. Genuine comment conversations and fast creator replies.
5. Minor metadata refinements.
6. Alternate-account interaction, which should be labeled low impact and optional.

## Response format

Lead with the safest best approach. Then provide the exact ready-to-post text and a concise rollout checklist. Separate policy facts from recommendations and attach official citations near policy claims. If the user asks to automate this workflow, automate drafting, analytics checks, reminders, or reporting only; never automate likes, comments, views, subscriptions, or account switching.
